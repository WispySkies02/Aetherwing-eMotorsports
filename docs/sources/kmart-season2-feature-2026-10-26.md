### 310 POINTS. ONE DEPARTURE. A CHAMPIONSHIP TRANSFORMED.

**How Hailey Bell went from tenth in the standings to the brink of a Kmart Auto Parts Series regular-season championship**

*October 26, 2026 | KMART AUTO PARTS SERIES CHAMPIONSHIP INSIDER*

**CHARLOTTE, N.C.** — Ten races ago, Hailey Bell was simply trying to establish herself in the Season 2 Kmart Auto Parts Series championship.

Today, she's the championship leader, mathematically guaranteed a place in the six-driver Chase and potentially just two points races away from securing the regular-season crown.

It's a remarkable reversal of fortune, built around one of the most significant points adjustments of the season.

The story begins with two other drivers.

Dean dominated the opening weeks, winning consecutive races at Daytona and Fontana. Jaxon then responded with back-to-back victories at Las Vegas and Circuit of the Americas, taking control of the championship.

Meanwhile, Bell's No. 15 StarClutch Racing Dodge slowly climbed the standings.

After opening seventh at Daytona, she fell to tenth following Fontana. By Las Vegas, she was back inside the provisional Chase field. At Michigan and Rockingham, she had climbed to fourth.

But she was still chasing.

On September 28 at Portland, Bell finished fourth and reached 223 championship points. Jaxon stood atop the standings with 310. Will had 288, and Carl had 236.

Then came the announcement that changed the championship.

Jaxon departed the series, and his 310 championship points were transferred to Bell.

Bell's official total jumped to 533. Fourth became first. A 65-point deficit to Will became a 245-point advantage.

**It was an extraordinary opportunity, but the racing still had to continue.**

And Bell has made the most of the opportunity since.

At Martinsville, she earned another 33 points. At Talladega, she added 40 more. Across those two events, her 73 race-earned points outpaced every other eligible full-time championship contender.

Her lead over Will increased from 245 to 281.

The distinction between the official standings and Bell's on-track performance is important. Of her 606 official points, 296 were earned through competition and 310 came from Jaxon's transfer.

That adjustment created her enormous lead, but her recent performances have strengthened it.

Now the No. 15 team's first major achievement is official.

**Hailey Bell has clinched her place in the Season 2 Chase for the Championship.**

With seven regular-season points races remaining and a maximum of 65 points available per race, no driver currently outside the top six can accumulate enough points to overtake Bell's existing total.

But the bigger prize is still available.

Bell enters the upcoming Iowa race with a 281-point lead over Will. She cannot clinch the regular-season championship at Iowa, but Mexico City on November 9 presents the first mathematical opportunity.

If Bell can leave Mexico City with at least a 326-point advantage over every eligible rival, the regular-season championship will be hers with five races still remaining.

New Hampshire on November 16 offers another credible opportunity.

For a driver who was tenth after the season's second race, that's an extraordinary position to occupy.

Yet the bigger challenge comes later.

The Kmart Auto Parts Series resets its championship points before the six-race Chase. A regular-season championship would provide Bell with 2,100 starting points, 25 ahead of the second seed.

Her enormous current advantage would disappear.

Six races, beginning at Darlington and ending at Homestead-Miami in January, would decide the overall title.

Jaxon's departure may have fundamentally altered Bell's championship trajectory. But she will still have to turn that opportunity into the biggest achievement of her season.

For now, Bell arrives at Charlotte for the O'Reilly Auto Parts All-Star Race with a guaranteed Chase berth, the championship lead and the possibility of claiming the regular-season crown before Thanksgiving.

**From tenth to fourth. From fourth to first. From fighting for the Chase to fighting for the regular-season championship.**

*And the season isn't over yet.*
